Quantitative Methodology & Boundary Disclosure

Parametric Value at Risk (VaR) is computed via the Variance-Covariance framework using 252 annual trading days and normal distribution quantiles (Z=1.645 for 95%, Z=2.326 for 99%). Conditional VaR (CVaR / Expected Shortfall) measures tail risk beyond the threshold. Diversification ratio reflects weighted individual asset volatilities divided by aggregate portfolio volatility. Stress testing incorporates non-linear cross-asset correlation breakdowns where historical diversification often fails during market panic.
